HIVE-FSA-009E - PROJECT CHIMERA

Difficulty: Advanced / Difficult
Genuine Flags: 4

SCENARIO
HIVE CONSULT is investigating suspected manipulation and unauthorized movement of confidential client evidence.
Investigators recover a confidential board audit document from the affected workstation. The document opens normally and appears legitimate, but forensic review indicates that additional evidence may be associated with the file.
Your objective is to recover FOUR genuine flags while distinguishing real evidence from misleading or decoy flags.
A file may be valid and still contain more evidence than the application shows.
